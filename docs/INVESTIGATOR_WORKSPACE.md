# TRACEVAULT V3 — PHASE 17: INVESTIGATOR WORKSPACE & UNIFIED INVESTIGATION UI

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 17 delivers TRACEVAULT's institutional **Investigator Workspace**, providing financial crime analysts, regulatory officers, and law enforcement agencies (LEAs) with a dense, evidence-oriented, and explainable investigation cockpit.

The workspace directly consumes the authoritative `UnifiedInvestigationResult` contract established in Phase 16, synthesizing multi-rail data from:
- **Ethereum & EVM Blockchains** (Peeling chains, rapid dispersion, fan-in/fan-out)
- **National UPI Banking Rail** (High-velocity transfers, mule funnel chains, merchant exits)
- **Geospatial Telemetry** (Physical impossibility velocity violations, jurisdiction anomalies)
- **VASP Attribution Engine** (Centralized exchange identification, deposit hot wallets, risk tiers)
- **Unified Investigation Graph** (Multi-rail nodes, directed edges, cross-rail off-ramp bridges)

## 2. Institutional Design & Architectural Principles

### 2.1 Visual Design Philosophy
The workspace strictly avoids consumer SaaS aesthetics, neon cyberpunk stylings, or gamified dashboards. Instead, it adheres to institutional design standards:
- **Dark High-Contrast Palette**: `#071018` root background, `#0b161f` surface, `#101c26` raised panels.
- **Clear Chromatic Rail Encoding**:
  - **Crypto Rail**: Cyan / Teal (`#24c7c9`)
  - **UPI Banking Rail**: Violet / Purple (`#a855f7`)
  - **Geospatial Telemetry**: Amber / Gold (`#f59e0b`)
  - **Cross-Rail Bridges**: Magenta (`#ec4899`)
- **Typography**: High-legibility sans-serif UI typography (`Inter`) paired with monospaced hash and coordinate formatting (`Consolas`, `SFMono-Regular`).

### 2.2 Forensic Terminology Standards
- **Investigative Risk Indicator**: Every score (0–100) and severity badge is strictly framed as an investigative risk indicator for prioritization, **never** as *"confirmed fraud"*, *"criminal score"*, or *"proof of guilt"*.
- **Evidentiary Standard**: Evidence items are cataloged as *"Structured investigative evidence suitable for review"* (**never** *"court-ready"*).
- **Non-Inferential Identity Boundaries**: Cryptocurrency public keys and UPI VPAs are analytical nodes. Cross-rail correlations are termed *"Analytical associations"*, **never** *"same owner"* or *"confirmed identity"*. Synthetic associations are explicitly badged `SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION ASSOCIATION`.
- **Temporal Integrity**: Timestamps are preserved strictly as emitted by ledgers and core banking switches. Missing timestamps are explicitly labeled `TIME UNKNOWN` (timestamps are **never** invented or backfilled).

## 3. Workspace Layout & Navigation Hierarchy

The workspace is hosted at `/investigations/:id` and `/investigations/:id/:tab` (with default tab `overview`), accessible via the primary sidebar.

### 3.1 Top Header Bar
- **Investigation ID Badge**: Monospaced identifier (e.g. `INV-004`).
- **Parent Case Reference**: Case ID link (e.g. `CASE-CROSS-RAIL-004`).
- **Subject Target Pill**: Target identifier and classification (e.g. `wallet: 0xvictim...0001` or `upi_vpa: traveler@mockupi`).
- **Provenance Indicator**: Source provenance (`LIVE_INDEXER`, `MOCK`, `SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION`).
- **Action Controls**:
  - *Copy ID*: Copies canonical investigation ID to clipboard.
  - *Export JSON*: Downloads complete raw JSON dossier.
  - *Re-run*: Triggers live re-execution of the orchestration engine.

### 3.2 Risk & Status Ribbon
A four-cell statistical ribbon providing immediate situational awareness:
1. **Unified Risk Score**: Continuous gauge (0.0 to 100.0) with severity pill (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).
2. **Confidence Score**: Deterministic heuristic confidence level (e.g. `88%`).
3. **Scope & Rails**: Active rails badge group (`CRYPTO`, `UPI`, `MULTI_RAIL`, `ALL`).
4. **Lifecycle Status**: Deterministic orchestration status badge (`COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, `RUNNING`, `FAILED`).

## 4. The 11 Institutional Workspace Tabs

### Tab 1: Overview
- **Executive Investigation Assessment**: High-level natural language summary synthesized from all domain engines.
- **Target Subject & Provenance Details**: Normalized target profile, parent dossier, and data lineage.
- **Multi-Rail Graph Coverage**: Entity count, transaction edge count, discovered paths, and bridge count.
- **Domain Intelligence Summary**: Crypto, UPI, and Geospatial quick-status cards.
- **Forensic Disclaimers Box**: Prominent legal boundary guidelines.

### Tab 2: Risk & Scoring
- **Unified Score Gauge**: Visual score presentation with severity indicator.
- **Multi-Rail Domain Risk Contribution**: Separate sub-scores for Crypto, UPI, Geospatial, and VASP/Cross-rail modifiers.
- **Deterministic Score Explanation**: Complete explanation generated by `RiskAggregator`.
- **Contributing Risk Signals Table**: Additive risk rules with technical identifiers, weights, and descriptions.
- **Confidence Assessment Rationale**: Methodological breakdown of factors contributing to score certainty.

### Tab 3: Unified Graph
- Interactive visual graph built with `@xyflow/react`.
- **Rail Filter**: Filter visible nodes by `ALL`, `CRYPTO`, `UPI`, `MULTI_RAIL`.
- **Risk Filter**: Toggle between all entities and elevated risk entities (≥50).
- **Interactive Inspector**:
  - Click any node to view entity ID, rail, type, risk score, tags, and metadata.
  - Click any edge to view transfer classification, volume, currency, and cross-rail correlation evidence.
- Distinct visual styling:
  - Crypto nodes in teal glow
  - UPI nodes in purple
  - Centralized VASPs in amber
  - Cross-rail bridge edges rendered as animated dashed magenta connectors.

### Tab 4: Timeline
- Chronological multi-rail event stream ordering crypto transfers, UPI transfers, location observations, and bridge events.
- **Strict Temporal Integrity**: Missing timestamps rendered explicitly as `TIME UNKNOWN`.
- Filter by rail (`ALL`, `CRYPTO`, `UPI`, `GEOSPATIAL`, `MULTI_RAIL`).
- Displays actor, target, amounts, currencies, transaction hashes, and location references.

### Tab 5: Intelligence
Sub-divided into domain-specific intelligence panels:
1. **Crypto Behavioral**: Peeling chain layering detection, rapid dispersion (fan-out) status, transaction counts, and volumes.
2. **UPI Fraud**: High-velocity funneling triggers, mule layering chain depth, total fiat volume, and banking switch telemetry.
3. **Geospatial Anomaly**: Physical envelope evaluation, observed travel velocity (km/h), origin/destination coordinates, and travel impossibility violations.
4. **VASP Attribution**: Disclosed exchange deposit wallets, attribution confidence, and Section 91 notice readiness.

### Tab 6: Cross-Rail
- **Visual Multi-Rail Flow Diagram**:
  `Crypto Source Deposit -> Bridge Provider (VASP/P2P Desk) -> UPI Destination VPA`
- Displays bridge confidence, audit log references, and transaction values.
- **Non-Inferential Legal Safeguard**: Explains that cross-rail associations reflect exchange transaction matching, not verified individual identity.

### Tab 7: Evidence
- Structured investigative evidence review schedule cataloging evidentiary records.
- Columns: Evidence ID, Category, Reference Hash, Confidence %, Source Provenance, Description.
- Filter by Category: `CRYPTO`, `UPI`, `VASP`, `CROSS_RAIL`, `LOCATION`.
- One-click *Export JSON* action for evidentiary handover.

### Tab 8: Reasoning Trace
- 16-step deterministic execution trace verifying the orchestrator lifecycle:
  1. Request Validation & Sanitization
  2. Subject Normalization & Scope Determination
  3. Investigation Plan Formulation
  4. Data Source Discovery & Provenance Tagging
  5. Live/Mock Isolation Check
  6. Crypto Intelligence Execution
  7. UPI Fraud Intelligence Execution
  8. Geospatial Intelligence Execution
  9. Unified Multi-Rail Graph Assembly
  10. Graph Traversal & Cross-Rail Path Evaluation
  11. Behavioral & Fraud Findings Synthesis
  12. Attribution Candidates & VASP Resolution
  13. Multi-Domain Risk Aggregation
  14. Chronological Timeline Construction
  15. Evidentiary Chain Linking
  16. Limitations Assessment & Result Finalization

### Tab 9: Limitations
- **Partial Execution Notices**: Triggered automatically when domains are omitted or data is unavailable (e.g. `INV-007`).
- **Comprehensive Disclaimers**: Itemized list of data freshness, coverage gaps, and false-positive caveats.
- **Statutory Notice Guidelines**: Clarification on regulatory mechanisms required for identity disclosure.

### Tab 10: Report
- 17-section formal compliance dossier.
- One-click browser print integration (`window.print()`) with print-optimized CSS.
- One-click full dossier export in JSON format.

### Tab 11: Audit
- Append-only forensic audit trail recording investigator actions, plan formulations, data isolation checks, and timestamped correlation IDs.
- Demonstrates zero sensitive credential leakage.
